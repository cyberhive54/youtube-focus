          Blocking starts only after you choose a plan.

          How YouTube Focus works

          1. Turn on Blocking. It activates the plan you choose.
          2. Normal keeps YouTube useful and removes distractions; Restricted allows videos opened from same-tab YouTube Search only.
          3. Study Mode allows only your approved channels and videos. Add one before turning it on.
          4. Daily limit counts active use. When it ends, the extra-time limit decides whether more time is available.

          Tip: the popup shows your current mode, time remaining, and the next action.
